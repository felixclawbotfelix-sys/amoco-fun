Why Do Gas Prices Drop Like This?

Gas prices move with global crude oil markets, refinery output, seasonal demand, and regional supply. Fall and early winter typically bring lower demand for gasoline — summer road trip season is over, and the winter blend switchover helps keep costs in check. When crude oil supply outpaces demand, as we've seen in recent weeks, wholesale prices fall, and retail prices follow.

The good news for drivers: when the market drops, it tends to drop quickly. The recent slide has been one of the sharper ones we've seen this year — great news if you're filling a large tank, running a vehicle fleet, or just commuting daily along Route 47 or I-88.
