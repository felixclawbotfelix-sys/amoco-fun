Driving I-88? The Best Gas Station Exit Is Just 6 Miles Away

Published August 10, 2026 · Amoco BP Elburn

If you're cruising the I-88 Ronald Reagan East-West Tollway and your tank is running low — or you just need a coffee, a snack, and a quick break — you're closer to a great stop than you think.

Amoco BP Elburn is just 6 miles north of I-88 at the IL-47 Sugar Grove/Elburn exit (Exit 109). It's a straight shot up IL-47 — no turns, no traffic to fight through — just a clean, fast stop and you're back on the road.

Why the IL-47 Exit Is Worth Taking

I-88 handles tens of thousands of vehicles every day connecting the Quad Cities to the Chicago suburbs. Whether you're heading east toward Aurora and Naperville or west through DeKalb and beyond, the IL-47 exit puts you right in reach of one of Kane County's most convenient fuel stops.

  • Regular, Midgrade, Premium, and Diesel — all grades available, 24/7
  • BP Amoco quality fuel with Invigorate® technology
  • Competitive prices — check live prices at amoco.fun
  • Full convenience store — snacks, energy drinks, cold beverages, tobacco, and nicotine products
  • Clean, well-lit, and fast — in and out quickly

Directions from I-88 Exit 109

It couldn't be simpler:

  1. Take Exit 109 off I-88 onto IL-47 North (Sugar Grove / Elburn exit)
  2. Drive north 6 miles — straight road, no turns
  3. We're on the right at 940 N Main Street, Elburn IL 60119 — Get directions on Google Maps

Total detour time from I-88 to the pump and back: roughly 15 minutes. Well worth it for a full tank, a fresh drink, and a clean restroom.

Open 24/7 — Whenever You're Passing Through

Driving overnight? Heading out before sunrise? Coming back late on a Sunday? Amoco BP Elburn is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. No "closed" sign here — just pull in whenever you need us.

Diesel drivers: we carry ULSD #2 diesel at the pump. Semi-trucks, pickup trucks, and diesel SUVs are all welcome around the clock.

The Smartest Stop on the IL-47 Corridor

Whether you're a daily commuter, a long-haul traveler, a contractor heading to a job site, or a family on a road trip — Amoco BP Elburn is the smart stop off I-88. Six miles from the exit, zero hassle, open all night.
